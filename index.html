Savings guide

Learn how to save money.

Start with one goal. Give it a dollar amount and a deadline, then work out what to put aside each month.

  1. Find room in your budget. List your take-home pay and regular expenses. Look for one expense you can cut or reduce.
  2. Build an emergency cushion. Set aside money for unexpected costs like an urgent car repair or a gap in income.
  3. Make saving automatic. Schedule a transfer after payday for an amount that fits your budget. Adjust it when your expenses change.
